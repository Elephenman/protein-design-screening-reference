## 1. 这篇到底解决什么（大白话）

在它之前，"用计算机凭空设计一个能贴住指定蛋白表面某个位点的新蛋白"要么靠**从大自然/展示库里大海捞针**（抗体库、DARPIN 库：无法指定要贴哪一块表面），要么靠**热点残基 + 现成脚手架对接**（要求靶面上正好有个能塞进去的口袋，且只用几个 privileged 侧链，把可能的结合方式限死在一小撮）。作者把问题重写成**两阶段搜索**：先用" disembodied （ disembodied 氨基酸）"把靶面上**所有可能的弱相互作用**穷举出来（可达数十亿个，存进一个六维空间哈希表叫 RIF，把复杂度从按交互条数线性/平方降到常数级），再把预筛过的 5 万条迷你蛋白骨架库**对着这张"弱作用力图"去接**（RIFDock），Rosetta 设计界面序列；然后**聚焦重搜**——把第一轮里真做出好接触的二阶结构 motif 抽出来聚类（约 2,000 条 motif/靶），再以 motif 为"特权模板"嫁接第二轮骨架。最终在 12 个蛋白的 13–14 个位点上全部拿到结合体（300 pM–900 nM），5 例复合物晶体结构与计算模型一致。它最重要的副产品其实不是结合体本身，而是**"近 50 万条设计 + 数十万条点突变"的带结局标签数据集**——一年后 [B09] 正是拿这批数据去考核"漏斗该用哪个判据"。

## 2. 遇到的难点，以及遇到问题时他们做了什么（作者自述，不是我们的推测）

1. **根本难点被明确列成两条，而且第二条是组合爆炸**。原文：**"there are generally no clear side-chain interactions or secondary structure packing arrangements that can mediate strong interactions with the target; instead there are vast numbers of individually very weak possible interactions"**；以及 **"the number of ways of choosing which of these numerous weak interactions to incorporate into a single binding protein is combinatorially large, and any given protein backbone is unlikely to be able to simultaneously present side chains that can encompass any preselected subset of these interactions"**。**应对**：不追求"挑对相互作用"，改成**先把所有弱作用穷举、再让大量骨架各自去凑子集**，用他们自己的攀岩墙类比：**"To illustrate our approach, consider the simple analogy of a difficult climbing wall with only a few suitable footholds or handholds distant from each other"**，落到流程上就是 **"Steps (1) and (2) widely search the space, whereas steps (3) and (4) intensify the search in the regions that show the most promise"**。**代价**：搜索空间是 **"tens of thousands of possible protein backbones"** × **"nearly 1 billion possible disembodied side-chain interactions per target"**，作者承认 **"Sampling of spaces of this size is necessarily incomplete, and many of the designs at this stage contained buried unsatisfied polar atoms"**。
2. **旧路线的失效方式被逐条点名**（决定他们不走回头路）：抗环对接 **"Computational docking of antibody scaffolds with varied loop geometries has yielded binders, but the designed binding modes have rarely been validated with high-resolution structures"**；热点法 **"for many target proteins, there are no obvious pockets or clefts on the protein surface into which a small number of privileged side chains can be placed, and guidance by a small number of hotspot residues limits the approach to a small fraction of possible interaction modes"**。**应对**：设计阶段**故意不用**天然界面信息——**"Some of the selected targeting regions overlap with the native interfaces, but no native interface information or native hotspots were used during the binder design process"**，连无天然配体结构的靶（**"For some targets (for example, CD3δ and VirB8), no structures of the native complex were available and there were no proteins known to bind at the targeted region"**）也照做。
3. **RIF 近似太粗，必须回补全原子评价，但全组合设计太贵**。原文：**"we found that evaluation of the RIF solutions was considerably enhanced by full combinatorial optimization using the Rosetta forcefield, which allow the target side chains to repack and the scaffold backbone to relax"**，紧接着 **"However, full combinatorial sequence optimization is CPU intensive"**；Methods 里给了量级：**"A severe speed mismatch existed between the docking methods (RifDock and focused search) and the subsequent full sequence design step"**、**"Although the docking methods can typically produce outputs every 1-3 s, the full sequence design can take upwards of 4 min"**。**应对**：造一个**只用于排序、不用于优化**的廉价代理打分器（Predictor）——**"To enable efficient screening of millions of alternative backbone placements, we developed a rapid interface pre-screening method using Rosetta to identify promising RIF docks"**，做法是**砍能量项 + 只留疏水氨基酸 + 少 rotamer**：**"Restricting to hydrophobic amino acids and considering a smaller number of side-chain rotamers than in standard Rosetta design calculations, together with a more rapidly computable energy function sped up the design time by more than tenfold while retaining a strong correlation with results after full sequence design"**、**"A stripped down version of the Rosetta beta_nov16 score function was used to design only with hydrophobic amino acids"**、**"Specifically, fa_elec, lk_ball[iso,bridge,bridge_unclp], and the _intra_ terms were disabled as these proved to be the slowest energy methods by profiling"**，目标时长 **"To remedy this situation, a step was designed to take about 20 s that would be more predictive than metrics evaluated on raw docks, but faster than the full sequence design"**。并**明确它的边界**：**"Note that the rapid design protocol here is used merely to rank the designs, not to optimize them; the raw, non-rapid-designed docks are the structures carried forward"**。**代价**：总成本仍然极高——**"CPU time requirements to produce 100,000 designed binders to be tested experimentally were typically around 100,000 CPU hours"**，且**"usually at least ten times as many binders were computationally designed than were ordered"**（底稿口径）。
4. **代理打分器怎么定"过/不过"？他们把阈值变成概率。** 这是本文方法论里最被低估的一步：**"With a goal threshold for each filter, each fully designed output can be marked as pass or fail for each metric independently"**，然后 **"Then, by binning the fully designed outputs by their values from the rapid trajectory and plotting the fraction of designs that pass the goal threshold, the probability that each predicted design passes each filter can be calculated (sigmoids are fitted to smooth the distribution)"**，再 **"From here, the probability of passing each filter may be multiplied together to arrive at the final probability of passing all filters"**；依据是 **"Based on the observation that these predicted metrics correlated with the values after full sequence design, a maximum likelihood estimator (a functional form similar to logistic regression) was used to give each predicted design a likelihood that it should be selected to move forward"**。**应对**的代价：这套"多过滤器概率连乘"**没有校准曲线/ROC**，只是用子集自证相关性。
5. **标准 Rosetta 设计造出"埋住的极性原子"等坏结构**。原文：**"We observed that application of the standard Rosetta design to the set of filtered docks in some cases resulted in models with buried unsatisfied polar groups and other suboptimal properties"**。**应对**：改协议，一次改四处——**"To overcome these limitations, we developed a combinatorial sequence design protocol that maximizes shape and chemical complementarity with the target while avoiding buried polar atoms"**：① 序列侧加天然片段 profile（**"Sequence compatibility with the scaffold monomer structure was increased using a structure-based sequence profile"**）；② 界面对相互作用加权（**"cross-interface interactions were upweighted during the Monte-Carlo-based sequence design stage to maximize the contacts between the binder and the target"**、**"To obtain more contacts between the binder and the target protein, we can upweight the energies of all the cross-interface rotamer pairs by a defined factor"**、**"In this way, the Monte Carlo protocol will be biased to find solutions with better cross-interface interactions"**）；③ 打包前直接删掉带不可满足埋藏极性的 rotamer（**"rotamers that contained buried unsatisfiable polar atoms were eliminated before packing"**）；④ 再用可分解伪能量项惩罚（**"buried unsatisfied polar atoms penalized by a pair-wise decomposable pseudo-energy term"**）。结果：**"This protocol yielded amino acid sequences that were more strongly predicted to fold to the designed structure"**。**代价**：他们自己限定只排除了"在任何上下文都成不了氢键"的 rotamer（**"only rotamers that cannot make hydrogen bonds in any context are excluded at the packing stage"**），所以埋藏极性/空腔问题并未根除。
6. **物理指标与肉眼判断不一致 → 现造一个新指标。** **"We developed a quantitative measure of packing quality in closer accord with visual assessment"**，即接触分子表面 CMS：**"which balances interface complementarity and size in a manner that explicitly penalizes poor packing"**。动机是 delta-SASA 会把孔洞当接触：**"If the sizes of the holes or cavities in the interface are smaller than the rolling probe, SASA cannot capture those holes and cavities and the real contacts are usually overestimated by the delta-SASA metric"**。做法是给三角形面积按对面最近三角形的距离做指数降权：**"For each triangle, the distance to the closest triangle on the other side was calculated and used to downweight the area of the triangle by the following equation"**、**"Then all the downweighted areas were summed to obtain the contact molecular surface"**，并**同时**用于快速预筛与全设计之后（**"We used this metric to help to select suitable designs at both the rapid Predictor stage and after full sequence optimization"**）。背景动机句：**"we noted after inspection that even designs with favourable Rosetta binding free energies, large changes in the solvent-accessible surface area (SASA) after binding and high shape complementarity (SC) often lacked dense packing and interactions that involve several secondary structural elements"**。**代价**：CMS 是**人为构造的堆积质量代理**，其降权公式里的衰减常数是设计选择而非物理量。
7. **第一轮设计"界面稀疏、缺多二级结构参与" → 不重跑全库，而是抽 motif 再聚焦。** **"we intensified the search around the best of the designed interfaces"**；流程是**"Next, it clusters these motifs on the basis of their backbone coordinates and rigid body placements, and then selects the binding motif in each cluster with the best per-position weighted Rosetta binding energy"**（聚类按 **"All the motifs were then clustered based on an energy based-TMalign-like clustering algorithm"**，**"Those motifs within a threshold similar score (default of 0.7) from the current cluster centre were removed from the unclustered pool and added to the new cluster"**、**"The best motif from each cluster was then selected based on the per-position weighted Rosetta binding energy, using the average energy across all the aligned motifs at each position as the weight"**），规模是 **"Using this approach, around 2,000 motifs were selected for each target"**、**"Around 2,000 best motifs were selected, and the scaffold library was superimposed onto these motifs using the MotifGraft mover"**；motif 之所以值得信，是因为 **"are privileged because they contain a much greater density of favourable side-chain interactions with the target than the rest of the designs"**；第二轮 graft 之后**"The remainder of the scaffold sequence was optimized to make further interactions with the target, allowing backbone flexibility through backbone torsion-angle minimization to increase shape complementarity with the target"**，效果为 **"Design Interface metrics following resampling were considerably improved over those from the broad searching stage"**。**代价**：图注也写明这轮的收益体现在"top 1% 结合体的 ddG/CMS 分布"上（**"Bar graph shows the distribution over the three approaches of the top 1% of binders based on Rosetta dd G and contact molecular surface values after pooling equal-CPU-time dock-and-design trajectories for each of the 13 target sites and averaging per-target distributions"**）——**评价指标本身是自洽的计算指标，不是实验结局**。
8. **命中率极低、且极度依赖靶面性质**。作者不隐藏：**"Only a small fraction of designs bind, and in almost all cases,"**；分靶差异是 **"Design success rates varied considerably between the different targets"**，极端对比为 **"For some (FGFR2 and PDGFR), hundreds of binders were generated, whereas for others (TIE2 and CD3δ), fewer than 10 binders were obtained from libraries of 100,000 designs"**（δ 与数字间的原文写法照录）；并给出**唯一一条全局规律**：**"Across all targets, there was a strong correlation between success rate and the hydrophobicity of the targeted region"**。**应对**：① 承认"必须再进化"，用 SSM + 组合突变库补亲和力（**"In almost all cases, a small number of substitutions increased the apparent binding affinity, and we generated libraries combining 5-15 of these and sorted them for binding under increasingly stringent (lower target concentration) conditions"**）；② 把"计算没找到、湿实验才找到"的突变当成方法反馈信号：**"The set of affinity-increasing substitutions provide valuable information to improve the binder design approach, as these substitutions ideally would have been identified in the computational sequence design calculations"**，并注意到它们偏好酪氨酸（**"Many of these affinity-enhancing substitutions were mutations to tyrosine"**、**"which is consistent with the relatively high frequency of tyrosine in natural protein interfaces"**）；③ 用 SSM 指纹反证模型界面（**"Designed binding proteins (cartoons) are coloured by positional Shannon entropy, with blue indicating positions of low entropy (conserved) and red those of high entropy (not conserved)"**、**"The core residues and binding interface residues are more conserved than the non-interface surface positions, consistent with the computational models"**）。
9. **SSM 有明确盲区，他们用第二、第三条证据补**。作者自陈：**"The sequence mapping data report on the residues in the design that are crucial for binding, but only weakly on the region of the target bound"**。**应对**：加竞争实验与生物学读数（**"We obtained binders to all eight target sites, and the binding affinities of the optimized designs ranged from about 1 nM or better for TrkA and FGFR2 to 860 nM for IGF1R"**、**"The receptor tyrosine kinase binders are monomers, and as such are all expected to be antagonists"**、**"We tested the effect of the cognate binders on signalling through TrkA, FGFR2 and EGFR in cultured cells"**、**"Strong inhibition of signalling by the native agonists was observed in all three cases"**），再加高分辨结构（**"High-resolution structures are crucial for evaluating the accuracy of computational protein designs"**）。
10. **BLI 信号太弱 → 换标签、换传感器化学，而不是放弃这些靶**：**"The binding affinities of TIE2 and IGF1R minibinders were low, and MBP-tagged proteins were used for the binding assay to amplify the binding signal"**；IR 因方向问题另换传感器：**"The binding assay for the IR designs were conducted with Amine Reactive Second-Generation (AR2G ForteBio) Biosensors with the recommended protocol"**。
11. **难靶（group 2 流感 HA 茎部）被解剖成三条具体障碍，并针对性给出化学变通**。原文列了三条：**"First, the group 2 HA stem region is more hydrophilic, containing more polar residues"**、**"Second, in group 2 HA, Trp21 adopts a configuration roughly perpendicular to the surface of the targeting groove, which makes the targeted groove much shallower and less hydrophobic"**、**"And third, the group 2 HA is glycosylated at Asn38, with the carbohydrate side chains covering the hydrophobic groove"**。**应对**：仍做，并结合去糖基化变体测：**"We used our interface design method to design binders to H3 HA (A/Hong Kong/1/1968), the main pandemic subtype of group 2 influenza virus, and obtained a binder with an affinity of 320 nM to wild-type H3"**、**"the binder has higher affinity for the N38D variant, which lacks this glycan, than for wild-type H3 HA (A/Hong Kong/1/1968) in biolayer interferometry assays"**、跨亚组结合用竞争实验定位：**"The binding to both H1 and H3 HA is competed by the stem region that binds the neutralizing antibody FI6v3"**；亲和力损失给出机理假设：**"The reduction in affinity is probably due to entropy loss of the glycan following binding and/or steric clash with the glycan"**。**代价**：野生型 H3 只做到 320 nM（全文最弱的受体酪氨酸激酶外的靶），糖基化障碍**没有被解决**，只是被量化。
12. **本文自己没解决的部分（作者原话）**：**"Furthermore, the design of binders to highly polar target sites remains a considerable challenge: the sites targeted here all contain at least four hydrophobic residues"**——即**这条漏斗有明确的靶面准入条件**，本文未给出绕过它的做法；这一空白后来由 [B14] 的自由扩散/部分扩散路线补上。

## 3. 方法与工作流程（可复现顺序；每步：输入 → 操作 → 判据/阈值 → 输出规模）

### 用了哪些方法（工具箱，以及为什么选它）

- **相互作用穷举**：RIF（rotamer interaction field，把十亿级" disembodied 氨基酸—靶面" favourable 相互作用存进六维空间哈希表，O(N)/O(N²)→O(1)）。
- **骨架来源**：自己预筛的 miniprotein 库（片段组装 / 分段组装 / 螺旋延长，50–65 残基、更大疏水核心），**先验证稳定性再进漏斗**。
- **对接**：PatchDock 低分辨形状匹配 → **RIFDock**（在 RIF 里做刚体取向网格精修）。
- **序列/界面设计**：Rosetta FastDesign（改进版：结构序列 profile、界面交互 upweight、埋藏极性 rotamer 排除 + 可分解伪能量惩罚）。
- **廉价代理筛**：**Predictor**（砍能量项的快速设计，约 20 s/个），再用"每个过滤器通过概率连乘"（sigmoid 拟合）排序。
- **堆积质量指标**：**contact molecular surface (CMS)**（三角形面积按对面最近距离指数降权求和）。
- **聚焦重搜（resampling）**：抽二阶结构 motif → 能量型 TMalign 式聚类（默认相似分阈值 0.7）→ 每簇取按位加权能量最优 motif（约 2,000/靶）→ MotifGraft 嫁接 + 柔性 backbone 精修。
- **实验端**：酵母表面展示 + 多轮 FACS（先 with-avidity 去弱结合者，再 without-avidity 递减浓度）+ 深测序算 **SC50** → SSM 全位置指纹 → 5–15 个有益突变的组合突变库亲和力成熟 → E. coli 表达 + CD/BLI → X 射线/cryo-EM。
- **没有用的方法**：MD、QM、自由能微扰。全文唯一与"模拟"相邻的是 backbone torsion-angle minimization 与 Monte Carlo 采样（属 Rosetta 优化，不做动力学/势能面）。

## 5. 结论——作者说了什么

1. **只用靶结构、不用热点/片段/天然界面信息**就能给 12 个不同形状与表面性质的蛋白设计出结合体：**"The design of proteins that bind to a specific site on the surface of a target protein using no information other than the three-dimensional structure of the target remains a challenge"** 被他们声称解决。
2. **"广搜 + 聚焦"的两阶段范式有效**：**"starts with a broad exploration of the vast space of possible binding modes to a selected region of a protein surface, and then intensifies the search in the vicinity of the most promising binding modes"**。
3. 结合体**小于 65 残基、超稳定**，经实验优化后达 nM–pM：见摘要句（**"Biophysical characterization shows that the binders, which are all smaller than 65 amino acids, are hyperstable and, following experimental optimization, bind their targets with nanomolar to picomolar affinities"**）。
4. **5 例复合物晶体结构全部与计算模型一致**（**"We succeeded in solving crystal structures of five of the binder-target complexes, and all five closely match the corresponding computational design models"**），并据此主张：**"the overall structure of the complex, including the structure of the monomer binders and the detailed target binding mode, are determined by the computational design procedure"**。
5. 数据集是方法学改进与 PPI 物理化学研究的起点：**"The datasets generated in this work-both the information on binders versus non binders and the feedback on the effects of individual point mutants on binding-should help to guide the development of methods for designing high-affinity binders directly from the computer with no need for iterative experimental optimization"**。
6. 应用与成本主张：**"Unlike antibodies, the designed proteins are soluble when expressed in E. coli at high levels and are thermostable, and hence could form the basis for a next generation of lower cost protein therapeutics"**、**"the ability to rapidly and robustly design high-affinity binders to arbitrary protein targets could transform the many areas of biotechnology and medicine that rely on affinity reagents"**。
7. 作者自限（三条，均原文）：**"However, there is still considerable room for improvement"**；**"Only a small fraction of designs bind, and in almost all cases,"**；**"Furthermore, the design of binders to highly polar target sites remains a considerable challenge: the sites targeted here all contain at least four hydrophobic residues"**。

## 6. 结论——我们读出了什么（以下为推断，非作者原文）

1. **这篇的"漏斗"其实是"自造指标 + 自证自洽"**：Predictor 用全设计结果训练、CMS 为贴合肉眼而造、聚焦重搜的成效用 ddG/CMS 分布来判定——**没有一个判据是被独立实验结局校准过的**。→ 对我们：任何"由同一套计算自造并自证"的筛子，进入漏斗前必须先在独立真值面板上过一遍（对应我们缺的 A 段考卷）。
2. **他们把"阈值"换成了"通过概率"，这是可搬的最重要一步**（第 2.4 条的 sigmoid 连乘）。这等于承认"单个阈值不可靠，只有分布上的通过可能性可靠"。→ 对我们：筛选漏斗里的判据（活性位点几何、折叠置信度、竞争反应可及性）应输出**概率 + 校准集**，而不是"过/不过"。
3. **成功率的决定因素是靶面性质，而不是设计算法**：**"Across all targets, there was a strong correlation between success rate and the hydrophobicity of the targeted region"** 与"数百 vs 少于 10"的同库对比说明**换靶比换方法更值钱**。→ 对我们：候选"反应/底物"层面的选择（哪一步化学事件最容易形成可测差异）应优先于堆方法；把靶面准入条件（这里是 ≥4 个疏水残基）这类"能不能做"的先验显式写进立项判据。
4. **"实验必须回来补计算"**：亲和力几乎全靠 SSM + 组合突变再进化拿到，作者明说这些突变"本应在计算序列设计里被识别出来"。→ 对我们：不要指望首轮计算给出最优，但**必须像他们一样把"计算漏掉的东西"结构化留档**（阳性 + 阴性 + 有益突变），否则下一轮没有考卷。
5. **"用第二/第三条证据补单一读数盲区"的模板**：SSM 说不清结合到哪块表面 → 用竞争 + 功能抑制 + 结构补齐（第 2.9 条）。→ 对我们：单一活性高低不构成结论，必须配"产物身份（LC-MS/MS）+ 定点失活 + 竞争物/底物谱"三类互不冗余的读数。
6. **他们没解决"极性/平坦表面"，这条空白是方法论的分水岭**：本文靠疏水驱动 + 大库硬堆通量；[B14] 后来用自由扩散 + 部分扩散补上。**我们应当把"哪一类问题由哪一代方法解决"当作漏斗设计的历史依据，而不是把某一代的阈值当普适闸门。**

## 7. 局限与失败（作者的 + 我们读出的）

- 作者承认：见第 5.7 条（成功率低、必须再进化、极性位点难）；以及采样必然不完整（**"Sampling of spaces of this size is necessarily incomplete, and many of the designs at this stage contained buried unsatisfied polar atoms"**）、埋藏极性排除规则很弱（**"only rotamers that cannot make hydrogen bonds in any context are excluded at the packing stage"**）、SSM 对靶面定位信息弱（**"The sequence mapping data report on the residues in the design that are crucial for binding, but only weakly on the region of the target bound"**）、H3 只到 320 nM 且去糖基化后才 28 nM、其余设计"尚未能解结构"（**"Although we were not able yet to solve structures for the remainder of the designs, the high-resolution sequence footprinting"**）。
- 我们读出：① **同一数据集的规模在本文里口径不一致**——摘要写"近 50 万设计与数十万点突变"，Determinants 段写"810,000 条 binder 设计与 240,000 条单突变"；靶点数也在 12/13/14 之间漂移（摘要 12、方法 13、结论 14）。引用这些数字时必须**指明取自哪一处**。② 没有报告 ROC/AUC 或阳性阴性对照校准，"强相关"只有子集自证。③ 命中率的分子/分母没有逐靶精确表（只给"数百"与"少于 10"的区间），任何"× 倍提升"的比较都不严谨。④ 与 [B09] 的"约 100 万条实验标注"不同源——[B09] 的回顾集是 10 靶约百万条，本文正文口径为 12/13 靶；两篇的**数据集边界不清**，引用时不可互相冒充。⑤ 全文无 MD/QM，界面能量学结论（例如"亲和力提升作用相对细微"）来自结构与 SSM，不是自由能。⑥ 本文的计算成本按 CPU 小时报（100,000 设计 ≈ 100,000 CPU 小时），与 [B09] 的 GPU 秒口径**不可直接换算**。

## 8. 迁移判断：哪些能搬、哪些不能搬

**可迁移（怎么用）**

1. **"先穷举弱作用、再让大量骨架凑子集"的两阶段结构**：我们的等价物是**先把催化几何事件（分步化学事件各自的必需接触/排布）离散化成"弱作用清单"，再让骨架/序列去凑**——而不是从"天然酶长什么样"出发做同源性召回。
2. **"稳定性预筛"这条独立闸门**：把 84,690 削到 34,507（约 41%）**先证明能折叠**，再进界面漏斗。→ 我们应把"可溶表达 + 折叠"设为与"催化假设"完全解耦的前置闸门（本轮的等价物：模型结构/折叠置信 + 表达实测）。
3. **"阈值 → 通过概率"的排序器**（sigmoid 连乘 + MLE 打分）：作为本库筛选漏斗的输出规范——**每个判据给出通过概率与校准依据，最终给联合概率与不确定度**，而不是二元 PASS/FAIL。
4. **自造指标的正当方式**：当现有指标与肉眼/已知事实不符时，明确定义新指标并给出公式与用途边界（CMS 的三角面元降权是范例）。→ 我们可以为"活性位点可用性"定义类似的可写公式的几何度量，但**必须注明它是代理量**，不许当活性闸口。
5. **"三条正交证据"结构**：SSM（位置级）+ 竞争（区域级）+ 结构/功能（原子与表型级）。→ 我们终池候选的最小证据集：**产物身份 + 定点失活 + 底物/竞争物谱**，缺一即降级为 UNKNOWN 或 METHOD_FAIL。
6. **把失败信息当交付物**：正文明写"这些有益突变本应被计算识别出来"并据此指导后续。→ 我们的 run.md 应固定记录"计算漏掉了什么"，与"命中了什么"同权重。

**不可迁移（为什么不能照搬）**

1. **本文全部判据是结合/堆积类（ddG、CMS、SASA、SC、形状互补），成功定义是"贴得住"**；目标酶要判三步化学事件与副产物分支。本库判据 §2 禁止把 binder 的 ddG/CMS/pAE/ipTM 类阈值当酶活闸口。
2. **"15,000–100,000 设计/靶 → 数百或 <10 命中"这类通量前提在我们这里不成立**：产物化学不能像酵母展示那样并行处理上万条序列，我们一轮只够个位数构建体。因此本文漏斗末端"排序取上端 0.1%"的**样本数最大化**思路必须替换成**信息量最大化**。
3. **"约 100,000 CPU 小时/10 万设计"不能拿来当我们的成本基准**：该口径只覆盖 Rosetta 对接与序列设计，不含其湿实验（展示库、深测序、合成），且与 [B09] 的 GPU·s 口径不可换算；我们已有自己的实测单价（MD、xTB 等），不得混引。
4. **本文无 MD/QM/自由能**，因此它不能为"用模拟判催化可行性"提供任何命中率证据；也不要用它的"亲和力 300 pM–900 nM"去暗示任何与催化速率相关的量级。
5. **靶面准入条件（≥4 个疏水残基）是他们的边界，不是我们的**：把这条当"立项可行性判据"照搬去评估酶反应位点是错的——催化位点的可及性由化学环境（电子态、金属、质子转移）决定，而非表面疏水性。
